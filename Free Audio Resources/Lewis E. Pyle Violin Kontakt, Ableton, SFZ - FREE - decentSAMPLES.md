The gorgeous sustained sound of this unique Pennsylvania-made violin (ca 1895) available in three convenient versions: Kontakt (**requires the full version of Kontakt (version 5.3.1 or later)**), Ableton Simpler/Sampler (**requires Ableton Live 9.7.1 or later**), and SFZ.

## Description

Features:

- One articulation: sustain with natural bowings (meaning the bowings have not been edited out)
- Three versions: 
    - Kontakt version is compatible with the full version of [Native Instruments Kontakt 5.3.1 (or later)](https://www.native-instruments.com/en/products/komplete/samplers/kontakt-5/).
- 
    - SFZ version tested with Plogue Sforzando
    - Ableton version: Compatible with Sampler or Simpler in **Ableton 9.7.1 or later**.
- Convolution reverb with reverb level knob
- Standard ADSR controls
- EQ controls
- Sample rate: 24-bit 48khz
- Library size: 44 MB
