This time we have something really special for you! A FREE vst instrument!

Subdivine Lite is a limited but still very powerful and useful version of our [808](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roland_TR-808) – type bass instrument. It has six great-sounding presets, one type of adjustable drive, partly modifiable ADSR and fully functional pitch bend. Enough to get a deep dive into the best sounding low end of modern urban music!

Check out this free vst version, get familiar with it, get used to it, fall in love with it, we’re sure you’ll want more!
