Cloudrum is a unique and relaxing Steel Tongue Drum with a range of 3 octaves. It produces wonderful tones, add color and a unique moderation to your music no matter the genre.

## System Requirements:

- 1 GB of free hard disk space.
- Windows Vista or newer, Pentium 4 or Athlon processor with 2 GB RAM.
- Mac OS X 10.7 or highe, Intel-based Mac with 2 GB RAM.
- AU, VST, AAX host application and professional sound card recommended. Stand-alone version included.
- AAX Windows version is not available in current version.

## Specifications:

- Size: 760 MB, 44.1khz
- DFD(Direct from disk)
- Two mic positions with stereo and mono modes.
- Open, Mute, Opened Release, Muted Release 4 articulations

## Technology:

- CPC (Customized Parameters Control) - any controller can be controlled by MIDI CC or Automation.
- Double - different samples at L & R channel.
- Each Cycle - Separate sample cycle for each note and velocity layer to improve humanization and sample utilization efficiency.
- Cycle * 3 Simulation - When toggled on, sample cycles can reach up to 12 (default is 4), which significantly enhances repetitive notes. No more robotic and machine-like sound.
