The scrooo is a polyphonic software synthesizer plug-in for Microsoft Windows (VST) and Apple macOS (VST/AU) based on a spectral formant synthesis architecture and written in native C++ code for high performance. Main features are:

- Up to 64 voices polyphony including portamento
- Three fully independent formant generator sections
- Three formant generation modes
- Two amplifiers with volume and panorama control
- Three envelopes (ADSR) with exponential slopes
- Three low frequency oscillators (LFOs) with tempo synchronization
- MIDI Learn – all parameters can be controlled by MIDI CC
- Double precision audio processing
- Plug-in supports Windows and macOS (32 bit and 64 bit)

Watch the [demo video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1OHKGMg5Az8) of the scrooo at Youtube!

On the two tracks ["detour2"](https://www.fullbucket.de/music/index.html) and ["scrooo"](https://www.fullbucket.de/music/index.html), the scrooo is the only audio source (plus some delay, reverb and slight compression).

the scrooo is subject of KVR's [One Synth Challenge #113 (OSC)](https://sites.google.com/site/kvrosc/osc-113-the-scrooo). 
Go to the [official OSC website](https://sites.google.com/site/kvrosc/osc-113-the-scrooo) and listen to the submissions of this great event!
