TAL-Chorus-LX is a 1:1 standalone version of the chorus implemented in TAL-U-NO-LX.

### Features

- Characteristics of the Juno-60 chorus.
- 2 different modes.
- Dry/Wet control.
- Volume knob.
- Only stereo channels supported.
