Driftmaker is a delay disintegration device that parses incoming audio, recalls certain parts and adds analog deterioration to the signal. It was designed to encapsulate faded, time worn the character of BlankFor.ms's debut on Puremagnetik - *Works for Tape and Piano *(out now on [Puremagnetik Tapes](https://puremagnetik.com/collections/tapes)).

Works for Tape and Piano immerses the listener in a sonic landscape where sustained piano tones bend and flutter as light travels through a prism. The centerpiece of this album's creation is a tape machine, which Tyler Gilmore aka BlankFor.ms uses to capture his world and warp it; sometimes speeding up time, sometimes slowing it down while often painting a warm, analog canvas, letting long forgotten recordings on old cassettes shimmer through to the foreground.

All presets were developed by [BlankFor.ms](https://blankforms.bandcamp.com/album/works-for-tape-and-piano).

**Minimum Requirements**
• OSX 10.8 or Windows 10 x64
• Audio Units or VST compatible audio host
