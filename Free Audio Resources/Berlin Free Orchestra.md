### What’s inside the Berlin Free Orchestra

You get strings, woodwinds, brass, and percussion. You get expressive solo instruments for main motifs and melodies, and you get ensembles for the broader brushstrokes and for sketching. You get auxiliary instruments like bass clarinet and contrabassoon, and you get some great pitched percussion including tubular bells and vibraphone. And all the instruments here are the same as in our flagship collections—flagship quality for free.

### About the Berlin Series

All instruments in the Berlin Free Orchestra are derived from flagship collections from the Berlin Series.

The Berlin Series is OT’s flagship series: The most comprehensive, integrated collection of sampled orchestral instruments available. All Berlin Series libraries were recorded with the finest musicians, in situ, on the same Teldex Scoring Stage in Berlin. You’ll find Berlin Series collections in the templates of the world’s top composers, from Los Angeles to Nashville, from London to Hong Kong.
