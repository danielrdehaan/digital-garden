## **PreBOX**

**size 10 MB / 11 MB / 5 MB**

**Preamp** modeler. Included 11 **preamp (mic and line).**
 All of them have their own character, transient response and dynamic. But freq. responses are fully linear. Cause it’s digital world and we can do what we want 
 Also it has filters from V76 circuit. Possible to use with all preamp models.
