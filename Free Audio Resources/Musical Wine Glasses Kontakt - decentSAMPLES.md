## Description

The musical wine glasses are played by rubbing a moistened finger around rim of a glass to make it vibrate.

Features:

- Compatible with the full version of [Native Instruments Kontakt 5.3.1 (or later)](https://www.native-instruments.com/en/products/komplete/samplers/kontakt-5/)
- Recorded in 48khz, 24-bit using a high-end audio interface
- Standard ADSR envelope controls
- 4 round robins per note
- 2 octaves
