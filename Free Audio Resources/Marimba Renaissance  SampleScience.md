**Marimba Renaissance** is a virtual instrument based on a damaged marimba recording that has been restored. A combination of audio engineering techniques has been used to make these marimba samples great: spectral repair, adaptative noise removal, manual retuning, and physical modeling. The result is a versatile instrument, rich in tone and easy to use.

![[SampleScience_Marimba_Renaissance_VST_VST3_AU_Screenshot.jpg]]

**Features of the plugin version:**

- 2 marimba sounds: standard and space
- Multi-LFO
- Room reverb
- Lowpass/highpass filter
- Low-CPU usage
- Amplitude range controls
- 3 voice modes: polyphonic, monophonic, and legato

**Decent Sampler version:**

![[SampleScience_Marimba_Renaissance_Decent_Sampler_Screenshot.jpg]]

The Decent Sampler version features the same sounds as the plugin version, but with different effects and sound-shaping capabilities. Decent Sampler is a free sampler VST/VST3/AAX/AU/AUv3 and is compatible with Windows, macOS, Linux, iOS. There's also a standalone version.

**Kontakt 5/6 version:**

![[SampleScience_Marimba_Renaissance_Kontakt_Screenshot.jpg]]

The Kontakt version has an amplitude and filter ADSRs as well as a delay, reverb, and chorus. The Kontakt 5/6 version requires the full version of Kontakt 5/6. It must be the latest updated version.

**Requirements for the plugin version:**

- Windows 8.1/10/11 or macOS El Capitan and up
- 116 MB of HD space
- 2GHz Quad-core CPU or better with 4 GB of RAM or more
- 64-bit VSTi/VST3/AUi Host

**Requirements for the Decent Sampler version:**

- The latest version of [Decent Sampler (Free)](https://www.decentsamples.com/product/decent-sampler-plugin/)
- 160 MB of HD space

**Requirements for the Kontakt 5/6 version:**

- The latest version of Kontakt 5 or Kontakt 6 and up
- It must be the full version, it won't work in the free Kontakt Player
- 274 MB of HD space
- except Android
