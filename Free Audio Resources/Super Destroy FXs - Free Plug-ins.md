**Hello.** Super Destroy FX is a collection of [Audio Units](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Audio_Units) and [VST plugins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Virtual_Studio_Technology) by Sophia and [Tom 7](http://tom7.org/).

These plugins are free for you and your friends to use. You can use them for whatever you like, but we'd appreciate it if you:

- [tell us](http://msg.spacebar.org/f/a/s/msg/disp/10) if you use them in a song that you release
- let others know where they can find this website
- [make a donation](http://destroyfx.org/donate.html), if you feel like these plugins are worth something to you

The [source code](http://destroyfx.org/source.html) for some of these plugins (and many unfinished plugins) is available for your perusal and modification. You can also download complete project archives on this page.

Please direct your comments, suggestions, and questions to the [guestbook / message board](http://msg.spacebar.org/f/a/s/msg/disp/10), or [email us](http://destroyfx.org/).

News and release infos are [here](http://destroyfx.org/news.html). Installation instructions are [here](http://destroyfx.org/faq.html) (in our FAQ). Answers to recurring questions can be found at our [FAQ](http://destroyfx.org/faq.html) page. Outdated versions of our software are archived in our [museum](http://destroyfx.org/museum.html).

Geometer is a visually oriented waveform geometry plugin. It works by generating some "points" or "landmarks" on the waveform, moving them around and messing them up, and then reassembling the wave from the points. Each of these three stages can be done in a variety of ways, combining to make millions of different effects. Geometer has a real-time display of what your settings are doing to the sound, and a prominent built-in help system to explain what each setting does.
