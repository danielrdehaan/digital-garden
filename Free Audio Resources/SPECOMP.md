Spectral Analog Compressor!

SPECOMP will compress overall signal and naive frequencies up to its magical circuit and tame whole range of sound.

This compressor based S-Type console compressor but heavily modified.

A simple meter to show overall gain reduction and frequency compression.

Plugin has simple compressor's settings as usual but magic is happening inside!

You will notice SPECOMP will put everything together, instantly!

- ANALOG OBSESSION LOGO: Oversampling. It will turn to red when oversampling is engaged. Also, show you a notification.

<u>**Available Formats**</u>

VST/VST3/AU | Mac 10.9 - 11.X

VST/VST3 | Windows 7 - 8 - 10
