Here is **AMPER**!

Mid/High gain metal amp for metal guitarist.

But, AMPER is little different than other metal amps. There are 2 different drive section. Sections are not parallel. Connected series.

Volume1 controlling pre-gain tube distortion before main pre-amp which is controlling by Volume 2.

Think like you have distortion pedal before your amp. But this drive modeled from tube amp distortion. Mid gain range to boost main pre-amp.

Tone control section based Sol**** amps.

Also, there is pre-tone shaper before preamp. Carefully designed for metal guitar to keep amp from annoying frequencies.
