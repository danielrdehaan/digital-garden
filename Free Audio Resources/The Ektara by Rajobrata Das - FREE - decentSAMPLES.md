## Description

The Ektara is one of the oldest single stringed instruments from India. This library offers a unique way to play it, along with 2 patches, one for the normal sound and one for its characteristics bend sound.

This Decent Sampler library works with the free **Decent Sampler** player plugin. Download it [here](https://www.decentsamples.com/product/decent-sampler-plugin/).
