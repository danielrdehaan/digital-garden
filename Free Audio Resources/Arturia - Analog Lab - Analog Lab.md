### Main Features

- 100 Presets created by expert sound designers from VCollection instruments and Pigments
- One simple interface with 4 macros: brightness, timbre, time, movement and 4 effects: 2 mod effects, delay, reverb
- Updated browser to find sounds quickly: filter by type and instrument
- Sound Store: 200+ Artist & Genre based sound banks
- New paid and Free content added regularly
- Automatic mapping of parameters for Arturia keyboards
- Parameters can be mapped easily to other MIDI controllers
