**Product Description:**

- Emulates hardware of Nintendo Gameboy
- 2 Square wave channels
- Sweeps on first square wave channel
- 1 Noise channel
- Waveform display
- Stereo output
- Free / Open Source
- Windows VST 32/64
- Mac VST/AU 32/64

**Version History:**
