## Sample Mode

- Load up ANY wav file, then, EB-FreakyTable c**onverts** the harmonics in that audio file into a filter shape, ready to be applied to any sound. Add offsetting, and other distortive effects to the signal for an even weirder sound. This thing is PERFECT for basses

![](https://public-files.gumroad.com/4esp4xy7wuaxivkh2cq2lgq25y69)

## Noise Mode

- Use the built-in Noise generator for Spectral-Sounding affects, bringing a bubbly effect to any sound. Use it with a delay for some EXTRA weird effects. Absolutely perfect for pads, or sounds that need that *SpEcTrAl SwEeTnEsS*

![](https://public-files.gumroad.com/156wj4wjz1rqjg0i2heu0tliktxd)

## Filter View / WavetablePosition

- View your Dry and wet signal, as well as the filter shape all in this window! Get precice and know exactly what you're doing to your sound with the handy visualisers

![](https://public-files.gumroad.com/4eif1kj5dxr2nk52qi2zise6w23l)
