### About the Collection

Our Blueprint libraries are designed to give you insight into our sampling process and the tools we offer in our commercial offerings. Some of the instruments are independent of our collection, whilst others offer lite versions of our commercial products. And others are sampling experiments that inform our main libraries, or an extra bit of time we had available during recording sessions.

Instead of leaving these gems on the cutting room floor, we offer them as free libraries, offering insight into our process– our Blueprint for creating our sample libraries. All available in the free Kontakt Player.

Our Blueprint Collection offers full NKS support, giving you seamless integration with Native Instruments’ Komplete Kontrol keyboards. The library also runs in the free Kontakt Player, activated through Native Access — no extra purchases required. Simply download Kontakt Player from the Native Instruments website to get started.
