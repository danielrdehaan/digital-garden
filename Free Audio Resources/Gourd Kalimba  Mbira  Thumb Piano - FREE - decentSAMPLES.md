## Description

The gourd kalimba is a lamellophone instrument from sub-saharan Africa. It’s played by flicking a series of metal “tongues” to make them vibrate.

Features:

- Two version: 
    - The Kontakt version is compatible with the full version of [Native Instruments Kontakt 5.3.1 (or later)](https://www.native-instruments.com/en/products/komplete/samplers/kontakt-5/)
    - The **Decent Sampler** version works with the free Decent Samples player plugin. Download it [here](https://www.decentsamples.com/product/decent-sampler-plugin/).
- Recorded in 48khz, 24-bit
- 4 round robins per note
- 2 octaves
