## Latest release

SBI preset files included in Windows release.

- [Ken Silverman](http://www.advsys.net/ken/), who wrote the original OPL emulation code back in 1998!
- The [DOSBox](http://www.dosbox.com/) team, for their amazing work.
- [Jeff Russ](http://jeff-russ.github.io/AdlibBlaster/), for his time and effort to get it building under OSX, and for building multiple releases.
- Trylle, who [kindly stopped by](https://github.com/bsutherland/JuceOPLVSTi/commits?author=trylle) to fix some issues and add features.
- Litaniast, who [contributed a bunch of SBI presets](https://github.com/bsutherland/JuceOPLVSTi/issues/36) (now included in release).
- Jeremy Penner, who [figured out the cause](https://github.com/bsutherland/JuceOPLVSTi/issues/34) of a long standing bug.
- Julian Storer, creator of the excellent [Juce C++ framework](https://www.juce.com/) which helps makes this possible.
- The folks at [shikadi.net](http://www.shikadi.net/Main_Page), who maintain a great wiki on reverse engineering DOS games. Includes details of the SBI format, and useful utilities for extracting instruments and music.
- [OPLX](http://www.oplx.com/), full of OPL programming documentation and a bunch of old-school DOS utilities.
- [Bobby Prince](http://bobbyprincemusic.blogspot.jp/2013/12/dosbox-vst.html), composer of the original Doom soundtrack.
- [Bedroom Producer's Blog](http://bedroomproducersblog.com/2015/10/28/juceoplvsti-vst/). We're in the top 50 free VST list.
- [Producer Spot](http://www.producerspot.com/best-free-vst-synthesizers-plugins-released-in-2015). Top 10 free synth plugins for 2015.
- [Icon](http://icon.jp/archives/11222). Nice review (in Japanese) by this music/art/tech culture site.
- [Scott Lander](http://blog.landr.com/10-free-vst-plugins-will-give-better-bass/). Top 10 free plugins for better bass, apparently.
- [Dune 2 soundtrack](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w6Lu0C_vZwQ) recreated in FL Studio by Mihai, who contributed the presets as SBI files.
- A [short demo](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yU0CWQ66Z28) of some classic FM sounds, showing how settings affect the sound.
- Some nifty original [dungeon music](https://dl.dropboxusercontent.com/u/28087823/LD34%20LandsOfLore/media/dungeon.m4a) created for a [game](http://ludumdare.com/compo/2015/12/16/dungeon-crawler-making-of/) by Christina Antoinette Neofotistou.
