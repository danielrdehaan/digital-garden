# Assignment: 16 Bars of Music with BBCSO Discover and LABS

# Objective:

Compose at least 16 measure of music using only sounds found in the Spitfire BBC Discover Orchestra or LABS series.

# Instructions:

If you have not already downloaded and installed both the BBCSO Discover or LABS instruments, start by doing that. If you need help, here is a guide: [Installation Guide (CCC): Spitfire Audio - BBCSO Discover ](Installation%20Guide%20(CCC)%20Spitfire%20Audio%20-%20BBCSO%20Di%201a5fb29beffe4bfea1abba84e6d168e0.md) You’ll also find at the bottom of the installation guide links to documents that give DAW specific-instruction for how to get the most out of BBCSO Discover.

Once you have both BBCSO Discover and LABS working within your DAW, compose at least 16 measure of music that uses sounds only found in BBCSO Discover or LABS.

**Do not use any other sounds, audio effects, or instrument other than those that you find in BBCSO Discover or LABS.**

> [!note]
> 

When you are done, upload a .WAV file of your composition to this week’s discussion on Canvas and be prepare to present your DAW project in an upcoming class.

# Grading:
