# Assignment - Design Two Weekly Habits

1. Start by identifying two **goals** you have for yourself that you would like to accomplish this semester. Your goals should,
    - Relate to your use of the laptop/electronics to compose, produce, and perform music;
    - Focus on specific skills or knowledge you would like to gain or improve upon;
    - And, require you to utilize the knowledge and skills you are learning in Foundation of Music I & II
2. Next, design two **habits** that will help you will repeat each week to help you achieve each goals. Each habit should be a clearly defined process that you can repeat or iterate upon each week.
3. Finally, determine how you will **document** and **evaluate** the progress you are making through your systems and how successful you ultimately are in achieving of your goals.

---

### Example:

**Goal:** Develop the skill to synthesize and program my own drum and percussion patterns similar to those used by (artist).

**Habit:** Each week I will select a single 8-bar excerpt from (artist’s) discography and recreate it from scratch.

**Documentation:** I will document my progress by,

1. Creating a short video each week that shows the original sample, followed by my recreation of it.
2. Compiling a document that contains…
    - A chart that tracks roughly how much time I spent each week
        1. Identifying each elements and transcribing its pattern;
        2. Recreating the exact sound and production style of each sound.
    - A list of all the resources I used to help me recreate each sample;
    - And, notes on the techniques that I used to synthesize each sound.
3. And, saving and sharing a presets for each of the sounds that I created while recreating each sample.

**Evaluation:** My progress will be measured both by successfully completing each week’s recreations and how similar my recreation sounds to the original. I will determine if I have successfully achieved my goal by comparing my first recreation against the ones I recreate at the end of the semester. I will compare them in the following ways:

- How quickly was I able to identify the individuals sounds and transcribe the patterns?
- How quickly was I able to recreate each sound?
- How similar does my recreation sound to the original?
