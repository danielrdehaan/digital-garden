# Assignment: Experiment with Audio Effects

# Objective:

Begin the process of familiarizing yourself with the concepts, features, functions, controls, and most importantly the sounds of common audio effects - like the [[Kilohearts Essentials]] 

Use  [Audio Effects - Explained](Audio%20Effects%20-%20Explained%2012cc1ca0af664b3f821805348221e43a.md) as your guide. You can either explore the audio effects included in your DAW or explore some of the free ones included in the [Audio Effects - Explained](Audio%20Effects%20-%20Explained%2012cc1ca0af664b3f821805348221e43a.md). 

# Instructions:

This assignment is more about experimentation that anything. So don’t worry about trying to make a loop or something that remotely sounds like a piece of music. Just try starting with different sound sources (e.g. a drum loop, a vocal sample, a synth melody, a bass line, etc.) then one at a time adding one effect and experimenting with how it alters that particular sound.

As you experiment, read any information provided in the [Audio Effects - Explained](Audio%20Effects%20-%20Explained%2012cc1ca0af664b3f821805348221e43a.md) guide and try and connect what you are hearing with your understanding of how each audio effect works. If something doesn’t make sense, try reading the user manual for the effect you are use, do a little research to see if you can’t find an explanation that makes sense to you, or if you can’t find anything useful on your own, email your instructor for help!

# Grading:

There will be no grades given for this assignment. So focus all your efforts on exploring this for yourself and bringing all your questions to our next class. Remember, the true test is how you can use this knowledge to unlock your own creativity.

**The deadline for this assignment is 11:59 PM this Sunday.** Feel free to reach out for clarification or guidance. Happy composing!
