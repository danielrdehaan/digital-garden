# Assignment: 16 Bars of Music with Speedrum Lite & Vital

# Objective:

Compose at least 16 measure of music using only [Speedrum Lite](https://www.notion.so/SpeedDrum-Lite-499eb951afed4bcd8513be1cee01e14f?pvs=21) and [Vital](https://www.notion.so/Vital-Synth-18fdef2599674b63923d4d7058c489a0?pvs=21).

# Instructions:

Download and install both Speedrum Lite and Vital (links above). Then review the suggested tutorials for each instrument.

Once you feel ready to explore, compose at least 16 measures of music using **only** Vital and Speedrum.

When you are done, upload a .WAV file of your composition to this week’s discussion on Canvas and be prepare to present your DAW project in an upcoming class.

# Grading:

|  | **Total Points:** | **10 Points** |
