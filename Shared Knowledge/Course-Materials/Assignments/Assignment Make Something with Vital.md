# Assignment: Make Something with Vital

# Prompt:

Create a short piece of music or sound design *using only* the Vital software instrument.

- Start with a completely empty DAW project.
- Begin from the **default Vital patch** (the raw saw wave).
- Do **not** use any presets. Every sound must be designed from scratch inside Vital.

# **Submission Requirements:**

- Export your work as a **.WAV file** with the following settings:
    - Sample rate: 48,000 Hz
    - Bit depth: 24-bit
- Upload the .WAV file to this week’s Canvas assignment.
- Along with your audio file, include a few sentences explaining your creative process (e.g., what you were aiming for, challenges you faced, techniques you explored).
