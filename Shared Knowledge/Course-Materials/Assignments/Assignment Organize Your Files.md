# Assignment: Organize Your Files

# Prompt

Following the guidelines presented in [Good Digital Hygiene](Good%20Digital%20Hygiene%20b4346c7d67fe459492147cefea1797da.md), organize all the files and folders on your laptop for the upcoming academic semester and your personal materials. It doesn’t need to be exactly like my system and doesn’t need to have all the backups that I do, but make it as organized, safe, and future-proof as possible.

# Materials to Submit

Bring your laptop to next class and be prepared to show your organization system.
