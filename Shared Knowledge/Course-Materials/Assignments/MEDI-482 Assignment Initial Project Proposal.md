# MEDI-482 Assignment: Initial Project Proposal

**Due Date:** Sunday, January 26 by 11:59 PM

**Assignment:** *Submit Initial Project Proposal*

**Instructions:**

- **Objective:** Introduce your capstone project idea and outline the foundational aspects of your intended project.
- **Content Requirements:**
    - **Project Title:** A concise and descriptive title.
    - **Project Description:** A brief overview (300-500 words) explaining the purpose and scope of your project.
    - **Objectives:** Clearly defined goals you aim to achieve.
    - **Significance:** Explanation of the project's importance and potential impact.
    - **Preliminary Resources:** List of resources, tools, or support you anticipate needing.
- **Format:** Submit as a PDF or Word document.
- **Submission Method:** Upload to the course’s learning management system (e.g., Canvas, Blackboard) under the "Assignments" section.

**Evaluation Criteria:**

- Clarity and coherence of the project idea.
- Alignment with course objectives.
- Feasibility and scope of the proposed project.
- Completeness of required sections.
