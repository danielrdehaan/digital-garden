# MEDI-482 Assignment: Refined Project Proposal

### **Week 2: Refined Project Proposal**

- **Due Date:** Sunday, February 2 by 11:59 PM

**Assignment:** *Refine Project Proposal with Feedback*

**Instructions:**

- **Objective:** Incorporate feedback received on your initial proposal to enhance and clarify your project plan.
- **Content Requirements:**
    - **Revised Project Description:** Updated overview reflecting feedback.
    - **Enhanced Objectives:** Refined goals with added specificity.
    - **Detailed Resources:** Expanded list of resources, including any additional support identified.
    - **Methodology:** Brief outline of the methods or approaches you plan to use.
- **Format:** Submit as a PDF or Word document.
- **Submission Method:** Upload to the course’s learning management system under the "Assignments" section.

**Evaluation Criteria:**

- Effective integration of feedback.
- Improved clarity and detail in the proposal.
- Demonstrated understanding of project requirements.
- Overall enhancement of the project plan.
