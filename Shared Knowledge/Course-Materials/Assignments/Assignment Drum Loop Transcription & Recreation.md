# Assignment: Drum Loop Transcription & Recreation

# **Objective:**

Transcribe and recreate one **4–8 bar drum loop** from an existing song as faithfully as possible. This exercise will strengthen your ability to identify rhythm, texture, tone, and groove by ear, and to accurately reproduce them in your DAW.

## **Instructions:**

1. **Choose a Song** – Select a song with a clear, loopable drum groove.
2. **Record the Loop** – Record or import a 4–8 bar section of the original song into your DAW.
3. **Align the Grid** – Adjust your DAW’s tempo and grid so the loop plays in sync with the project tempo.
4. **Recreate the Beat** – Using virtual instruments, samples, or synthesized sounds, recreate the drums and percussion **as closely as possible** to the original—paying attention to rhythm, tone, and dynamics.
5. **Document Your Work** –
    - Take a **screenshot of your piano roll or MIDI editor** showing the transcription.
    - Export a **.wav file** of your recreated drum loop.
    - Include a **YouTube link** to the original song.

# **Submission:**

Upload the following to Canvas (or your class folder):

- YouTube link to the original song
- Screenshot of your drum transcription
- `.wav` file of your recreated loop

# **Evaluation Criteria:**

- Accuracy of rhythm and timing
- Attention to tone and sound design
- Faithfulness to the original groove
- Organization, clarity, and completeness of submission materials
