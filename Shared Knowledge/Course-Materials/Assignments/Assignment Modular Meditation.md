# Assignment: Modular Meditation

### **Assignment Overview**

In this assignment, you will engage with the principles of modular synthesis by creating a custom patch using VCV Rack. Titled "Modular Meditation," this task is designed to deepen your understanding of the concepts introduced in this week's class through hands-on experimentation and creative application.

### **Objectives**

- **Apply** the theoretical concepts of modular synthesis in a practical setting.
- **Develop** proficiency in using VCV Rack to create and manipulate audio patches.
- **Demonstrate** creativity in exploring sound design and signal flow.
- **Enhance** technical skills in recording and presenting modular setups.

### **Assignment Instructions**

1. **Concept Review:**
    
    Revisit the key concepts discussed in this week's class. Ensure you have a clear understanding of the topics to be explored in your patch.
    
2. **Patch Creation:**
    - **Time Allocation:** Spend **1 hour** designing and building your patch in VCV Rack.
    - **Exploration:** Your patch should **explore and illustrate** the concepts introduced in class. This could involve specific modules, signal paths, modulation techniques, or any other relevant elements.
    - **Creativity:** While adhering to the concepts, feel free to experiment and incorporate your unique creative ideas to make the patch engaging and original.
3. **Screen Capture Recording:**
    - **Video Length:** Create a short screen capture video demonstrating your patch in action.
    - **Audio:** Ensure that the audio from your patch is clearly audible in the recording.
    - **Narration (Optional):** You may include a brief narration explaining the features of your patch and how it relates to the class concepts.
4. **File Preparation:**
    - **VCV Rack File:** Save your completed patch as a VCV Rack project file.
    - **Screen Capture:** Ensure your video file is in a commonly accepted format (e.g., MP4, MOV).
    - **File Naming Standard:** Name your files using the following format:**Replace `##` with the current week number and `Your-Name` with your full name.***Example:* `SP25_MEDI-481_Week-05_John-Doe_Modular-Meditation.mp4`
        - `SP25_MEDI-481_Week-##_Your-Name_Modular-Meditation.mp4`
        - `SP25_MEDI-481_Week-##_Your-Name_Modular-Meditation.vcv`
5. **Submission:**
    - Upload both the **screen capture video** and the **VCV Rack project file** to [Specify Submission Platform].
    - Ensure that your files follow the **File Naming Standard** outlined above.

### **Submission Requirements**

- **Screen Capture with Audio:** A video file showcasing your VCV Rack patch in action.
- **VCV Rack File:** The project file of your completed patch.
- **File Naming:** Adhere to the specified naming convention:
    - `SP25_MEDI-481_Week-##_Your-Name_Modular-Meditation.mp4`
    - `SP25_MEDI-481_Week-##_Your-Name_Modular-Meditation.vcv`
- **Deadline:** All submissions must be uploaded by **Sunday at 11:59 PM**. Late submissions may be subject to point deductions as per the course policy.

### **Evaluation Criteria**

Your assignment will be evaluated based on the following:

- **Understanding of Concepts (30%):** How well your patch demonstrates the week's class concepts.
- **Creativity and Originality (25%):** The uniqueness and creative application of modular synthesis techniques.
- **Technical Execution (25%):** The functionality and sound quality of the patch, including signal flow and module integration.
- **Presentation (10%):** Clarity and quality of the screen capture video and audio.
- **Compliance (10%):** Adherence to submission guidelines and deadlines
