# Assignment: Practice Mastering

# Objective:

Begin the process of familiarizing yourself with the concepts, tools, and workflows and mastering.

# Instructions:

For this assignment, begin by reviewing [Mastering Overview](Mastering%20Overview%2023fb22f501444ff5bb2d6cbacc1b86ec.md) and then apply that knowledge by attempting to master one of your own songs or one of the songs provided in the [Audio Mixing: The Basic Concepts & Workflows](Audio%20Mixing%20The%20Basic%20Concepts%20&%20Workflows%20cc739f124cf14bc99dfe1403adf9d396.md).

# Grading:

There will be no grades given for this assignment. So focus all your efforts on exploring this for yourself and bringing all your questions to our next class. Remember, the true test is how you can use this knowledge to unlock your own creativity.

**The deadline for this assignment is 11:59 PM this Sunday.** Feel free to reach out for clarification or guidance. Happy composing!
