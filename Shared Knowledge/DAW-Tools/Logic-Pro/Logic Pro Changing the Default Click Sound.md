# Logic Pro: Changing the Default Click Sound

> [!note]
> [UREI Click 48k WAV.wav.zip](Digital%20Garden/Shared%20Knowledge/Attachments/Logic%20Pro%20Changing%20the%20Default%20Click%20Sound/UREI_Click_48k_WAV.wav.zip)

1. Open Logic’s *Mixer*, and show All tracks.

![Screen Shot 2022-05-02 at 2.42.07 PM.png](Screen_Shot_2022-05-02_at_2.42.07_PM.png)

1. Find the “Click” track and replace the existing Klopfgeist instrument with Logic’s Quick Sampler (mono).
2. Rename the track back to “Click”

![Screen Shot 2022-05-02 at 2.44.10 PM.png](Screen_Shot_2022-05-02_at_2.44.10_PM.png)

1. Load the downloaded UREI CLICK.WAV file into the newly added Quick Sampler and set the Root Key to `C5` then reduce the Volume to `-6dB`

![Screen Shot 2022-05-02 at 2.46.48 PM.png](Screen_Shot_2022-05-02_at_2.46.48_PM.png)

1. Next, open your Project Settings > Metronome…

![CleanShot 2024-04-18 at 10.09.20@2x.png](CleanShot_2024-04-18_at_10.09.202x.png)

1. Adjust your metronome settings to match the following image.
    
    ![CleanShot 2024-04-18 at 10.14.50@2x.png](CleanShot_2024-04-18_at_10.14.502x.png)
    
2. Add a track on the timeline for the Click Channel Strip

![Screen Shot 2022-05-02 at 2.48.28 PM.png](Screen_Shot_2022-05-02_at_2.48.28_PM.png)

1. Set the Click tracks output to be Mono

![Screen Shot 2022-05-02 at 2.49.53 PM.png](Screen_Shot_2022-05-02_at_2.49.53_PM.png)

1. Now you can export a Mono UREI Click audio file just like you would for any other STEM or individual audio file just be sure to not check “Include volume/pan automations” in the export window.
    
    ![CleanShot 2024-11-13 at 09.54.05@2x.png](CleanShot_2024-11-13_at_09.54.052x.png)
