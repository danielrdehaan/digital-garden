# Cubase: Changing the Default Click Sound

> [!note]
> [UREI Click 48k WAV.wav.zip](Digital%20Garden/Shared%20Knowledge/Attachments/Cubase%20Changing%20the%20Default%20Click%20Sound/UREI_Click_48k_WAV.wav.zip)

1) Choose “Metronome Setup...” from the Transport Menubar dropdown menu.

![Screen Shot 2022-05-02 at 2.55.44 PM.png](Screen_Shot_2022-05-02_at_2.55.44_PM.png)

2) Navigate to the “Click Sounds” tab.

![Screen Shot 2022-05-02 at 2.57.24 PM.png](Screen_Shot_2022-05-02_at_2.57.24_PM.png)

3)Then set all the Custom Sounds to the downloaded UREI Click .WAV file. 

![Screen Shot 2022-05-02 at 2.58.45 PM.png](Screen_Shot_2022-05-02_at_2.58.45_PM.png)

4) To create a visible Click track, first set your locators to the full duration of your project, then click the dropdown menu on your Signature track and select “Render Audio Click between Locators.

![Screen Shot 2022-05-02 at 3.08.15 PM.png](Screen_Shot_2022-05-02_at_3.08.15_PM.png)

5) To Export a Mono .WAV file. Open the “Audio Mixdown...” window, select your Click track and set it to export as a “Mono Mixdown”

![Screen Shot 2022-05-02 at 3.06.07 PM.png](Screen_Shot_2022-05-02_at_3.06.07_PM.png)
