# SAM Degree Maps

# Description

During the 2024-2025 academic year, the new School of Audio and Music was tasked with revising all its degree offering. To help guide our internal discussions I created maps that traced all the prerequisite pathways across all the new SAM degrees to help us identity and create natural synergies and places for collaboration between our degrees while also discovering any issues with the prerequisites of each course
