# Our Habits This Semester

> [!note]
> **RULE SEVEN: The only rule is work. If you work, it will lead to something. It’s the people who do all of the work all of the time who eventually catch on to things.**

`This semester you will each be responsible for`

- Setting your own goals;
- Designing systems for achieving those goals;
- Documenting your work;
- Tracking your progress;
- And, determining how you will evaluate your success in achieving your goals.

`The early part of the semester we'll focus on,`

- Goal setting;
- Systems design;
- Documentation strategies;
- Research methods;
- Learning habits.

`Towards the end of the semester we will be focused more on,`

- Learning from each other;
- Providing feedback and guidance;
- And, clarifying advanced topics and skills

`By the end of this semester,`our goal is to become better producers/composers and establish the skills and habits to continue developing ourselves beyond this class.
