# Learn your DAW + Expand Your Sound Pallete

# Prompt

Take some time to make sure you have a good understanding of how to use your chosen DAW and explore the sounds that it includes. You may also want to add some new sounds to your tool kit. I’d recommend taking a look at [[The Free Composer/Producer Toolkit & Beyond ✨]] for some great free stuff.

# Resources

- [LinkedIn Learning](https://www.linkedin.com/learning/?u=2243042) (which you can access by logging into your Columbia email at [www.office.com](https://www.office.com) and then clicking on the icon in the top left corner) has some great courses on most of the popular DAWs.

![LinkedInLearning.gif](LinkedInLearning.gif)

- YouTube is, of course, another great place to learn about your DAW
- Nothing is better is a better source for learning about your DAW than the user manual. Most can be found on the manufactures website.
- If you are having trouble finding good resources, send me an email or ask your peers on our [Discord](https://discord.gg/HfDG3tTg) server

# Evaluation

There will be no grade for this assignment. Still, you will thank yourself for taking advantage of this opportunity to refresh or establish your foundational knowledge of your DAW and related tools.
