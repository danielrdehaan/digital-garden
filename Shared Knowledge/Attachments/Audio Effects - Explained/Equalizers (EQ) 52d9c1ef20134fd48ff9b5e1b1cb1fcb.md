# Equalizers (EQ)

Equalizers (EQ) come in many different flavors and are useful at virtually every process of music recording, production, mixing, and mastering. All EQs allow us to shape the tone, or frequency characteristic of a sound. Checkout the video below for a good overview of all the various types of EQ you may encounter.
