# Amplitude Modulation (AM/Tremolo)

Amplitude modulation (AM), or tremolo, modulates the amplitude of one audio signal (the carrier) with the volume of another audio signal (the modulator). Typically the modulator is a Low-Frequency Oscillator (LFO) moving slower than twenty times per second (< 20Hz) so as to embed a rhythmic pulse onto the source sound.
