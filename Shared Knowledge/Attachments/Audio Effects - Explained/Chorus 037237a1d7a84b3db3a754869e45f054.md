# Chorus

The chorus effect is achieved by blending the original sound with a slightly delayed and pitch-shifted version of itself - kind of like a choir all singing the same melody.

Checkout these articles for more information on Chorus, Phasers, and Flangers:

- [Technical Explanation of Phasers and Flangers](https://www.soundonsound.com/sound-advice/how-phasers-work)
- [Understanding Chorus, Flangers, and Phasers in Audio Production](https://www.izotope.com/en/learn/understanding-chorus-flangers-and-phasers-in-audio-production.html)
