# Limiter

Similar to a compressor, a limit controls how loud a sound is allowed to get. However, you’ll notice that unlike a compressor, a limiter does not have a `ratio` control. That is because a limiter does not allow any signal to go beyond whatever its `ceiling` or `threshold` level is set at.
