# Installation Guide (CCC): Spitfire Audio - BBCSO Discover

> [!note]
> 

![](https://youtu.be/1v-2IyTtSLY)

# This is a free tool! Don’t panic.

The BBCSO Discover Library by Spitfire Audio is a **free** and easy-to-use virtual-instrument plugin that  that puts the sounds of the BBC Symphonic Orchestra at your finger tips. The plugin is compatible with any major music creation software (e.g. Logic Pro, Ableton, Reaper).

# Installation

1. Begin by navigating to [Spitfire's website](https://www.spitfireaudio.com/shop/a-z/bbc-symphony-orchestra-discover/) and adding the BBCSO Discover to your cart.
    
    ![CleanShot 2023-10-11 at 11.15.49.png](Digital%20Garden/Shared%20Knowledge/Attachments/Installation%20Guide%20(CCC)%20Spitfire%20Audio%20-%20BBCSO%20Di/CleanShot_2023-10-11_at_11.15.49.png)
    
2. Then go to your cart and begin the checkout process.
    
    ![CleanShot 2023-10-11 at 11.17.01.png](Digital%20Garden/Shared%20Knowledge/Attachments/Installation%20Guide%20(CCC)%20Spitfire%20Audio%20-%20BBCSO%20Di/CleanShot_2023-10-11_at_11.17.01.png)
    
3. Complete the checkout process by either signing in to your existing Spitfire Audio account, or creating a new one.
    
    ![Screen Shot 2021-10-13 at 7.56.07 AM.png](Digital%20Garden/Shared%20Knowledge/Attachments/Installation%20Guide%20(CCC)%20Spitfire%20Audio%20-%20BBCSO%20Di/Screen_Shot_2021-10-13_at_7.56.07_AM.png)
    
4. After successfully creating/logging in to your account, confirm your order.
    
    ![Screen Shot 2021-10-13 at 7.57.41 AM.png](Digital%20Garden/Shared%20Knowledge/Attachments/Installation%20Guide%20(CCC)%20Spitfire%20Audio%20-%20BBCSO%20Di/Screen_Shot_2021-10-13_at_7.57.41_AM.png)
    
5. Next, you'll need to install the Spitfire Audio App on your computer (download link for [Mac](https://www.spitfireaudio.com/library-manager/download/mac/) or [Windows](https://www.spitfireaudio.com/library-manager/download/win/)).
6. Complete the installation process for the Spitfire Audio App.
7. Once the installation has finished, open the Spitfire Audio App.
8. When the app first launches it will ask you to determine the default location for any related resources it may need to install. You can leave this set to the default location, or if you already have a specific location that you install all your instrument/sample libraries you can set it to that path and click "Finish".
    
    ![Screen Shot 2021-10-13 at 8.11.12 AM.png](Digital%20Garden/Shared%20Knowledge/Attachments/Installation%20Guide%20(CCC)%20Spitfire%20Audio%20-%20BBCSO%20Di/Screen_Shot_2021-10-13_at_8.11.12_AM.png)
    
9. Next, click to install the BBCSO Discover Orchestra library.
    
    ![Screen Shot 2021-10-13 at 8.14.20 AM.png](Digital%20Garden/Shared%20Knowledge/Attachments/Installation%20Guide%20(CCC)%20Spitfire%20Audio%20-%20BBCSO%20Di/Screen_Shot_2021-10-13_at_8.14.20_AM.png)
    
10. Then click, "Download".
    
    ![Screen Shot 2021-10-13 at 8.15.29 AM.png](Digital%20Garden/Shared%20Knowledge/Attachments/Installation%20Guide%20(CCC)%20Spitfire%20Audio%20-%20BBCSO%20Di/Screen_Shot_2021-10-13_at_8.15.29_AM.png)
    
11. While that is downloading, Spitfire has another collection of free virtual instruments called "LABS" that you may also be interested in adding to your collection. You can download any of these instruments by navigating to the LABS tab within the Spitfire Audio App. 
    
    ![Screen Shot 2021-10-13 at 8.18.59 AM.png](Digital%20Garden/Shared%20Knowledge/Attachments/Installation%20Guide%20(CCC)%20Spitfire%20Audio%20-%20BBCSO%20Di/Screen_Shot_2021-10-13_at_8.18.59_AM.png)
    
12. Then simply just click the "install" button for any instrument you would like to have. 
    
    ![Screen Shot 2021-10-13 at 8.19.35 AM.png](Digital%20Garden/Shared%20Knowledge/Attachments/Installation%20Guide%20(CCC)%20Spitfire%20Audio%20-%20BBCSO%20Di/Screen_Shot_2021-10-13_at_8.19.35_AM.png)
    
13. Follow the links below to finish setting up your new BBCSO Discover instruments for your specific DAW.
    - [Logic Pro](https://www.notion.so/Getting-Started-BBCSO-Discover-Logic-Pro-ec709054f4fc451f879414e1f0a26b27?pvs=21)
    - [Ableton](https://www.notion.so/Getting-Started-BBCSO-Discover-Ableton-Live-10-3fa6643583d9408ebc57f43ef588a390?pvs=21)
    - [Cubase](Getting%20Started%20BBCSO%20Discover%20-%20Cubase%200671cb4d1d9e4812885f345a9425653e.md)
    - [Reaper](https://www.notion.so/Getting-Started-BBCSO-Discover-Reaper-f7b0cad9bf394a72bec6c78719291182?pvs=21)
    - [GarageBand](https://www.notion.so/Getting-Started-BBCSO-Discover-GarageBand-d1bf390fab424618853d0c063f358dae?pvs=21)
