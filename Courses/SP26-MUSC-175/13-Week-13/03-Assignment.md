# Assignment - Week 13: Portfolio Draft

**Due:** Sunday, May 3, 2026 @ 11:59 PM

## Overview

Submit a draft of your Final Project evidence portfolio. This draft will receive feedback before your final submission.

## Deliverables

Submit to Canvas:

### 1. Written Reflection Draft

Draft your 500-750 word reflection:

**Minimum for draft:** 300+ words covering:
- Your two habits and why you chose them
- Key moments in your journey

*Mark sections that are incomplete with [TO DO]*

### 2. Evidence Collection (At Least 50%)

Organize your evidence so far:

**Habit 1:**
- [ ] Baseline evidence (Weeks 1-2)
- [ ] Mid-semester evidence (Weeks 6-8)
- [ ] Recent evidence (Weeks 11-13)

**Habit 2:**
- [ ] Baseline evidence (Weeks 1-2)

Create your submission folder following this structure:

```

├── 01_Written_Reflection_DRAFT.pdf

├── 03_Habit2_Evidence/
```

Your weekly documentation continues—this is one of your final check-ins before the portfolio!
