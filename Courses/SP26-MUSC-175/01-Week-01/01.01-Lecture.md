# Lecture - Week 01: Introduction & Habits Framework

## Overview

Welcome to Applied Music II! This week we establish the foundation for the semester—understanding how consistent habits lead to meaningful growth as musicians and producers.

- Why systems beat goals
- Identity-based habit formation

1. **Syllabus Q&A** (10 min)

   - Draft two potential habits
   - Pair share and refine

3. **Resource Tour** (20 min)
