# Lecture - Week 14: Portfolio Workshop

## Overview

This week is dedicated to completing your Evidence Portfolios. Based on feedback from your drafts, you'll refine your materials, strengthen your reflections, and prepare for final presentations.

## Topics

### Draft Feedback Review
- Common themes from portfolio drafts
- Addressing frequent issues
- Raising the bar on quality

### Strengthening Your Evidence
- What makes evidence compelling?
- Before/after comparisons that show growth
- Process documentation that tells a story
- Quality over quantity

### Writing Better Reflections
- Moving beyond description to insight
- Connecting habits to growth
- Being honest about challenges
- Looking forward: what's next?

### Presentation Preparation
- Structuring your 5-7 minute presentation
- What to show vs. what to tell
- Technical setup and preparation
- Practice strategies

### Looking Ahead
- What happens after this course?
- Continuing your habit practice
- Resources for ongoing learning
- Staying connected

## Session Structure

Today is primarily a **working session**:

| Time | Activity |
|------|----------|
| 0:00-0:20 | Feedback review and Q&A |
| 0:20-1:00 | Individual work time |
| 1:00-1:20 | Progress check-in and presentation prep |

## In-Class Activities

1. **Feedback Implementation** (40 min)
   - Work on addressing feedback from portfolio drafts
   - Instructor available for individual questions

2. **Peer Review** (20 min)
   - Exchange updated portfolios with a partner
   - Fresh eyes catch new issues
   - Focus on clarity and completeness

3. **Presentation Practice** (optional)
   - Run through your presentation
   - Time yourself
   - Get feedback on delivery
