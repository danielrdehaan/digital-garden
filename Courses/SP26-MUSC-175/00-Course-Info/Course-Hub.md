## The Habit System

This semester you'll design and maintain **two weekly habits** that support your growth as a musician. See [[Habits-Framework]] for complete details.

**Weekly Structure:**
- **Monday:** Lecture/Demonstration
- **Wednesday:** Alternates between Studio (odd weeks) and Feedback (even weeks)
- **Sunday:** Assignment Due (11:59 PM)

**Final Project:** Evidence Portfolio showcasing your habit growth (see [[Final-Project-Guide]])

## Wednesday Session Types

### Studio Sessions (Odd Weeks: 1, 3, 5, 7, 9, 11, 13)
Hands-on work time applying lecture concepts.
- **Solo** (Weeks 1, 5, 9, 13): Individual focused practice
- **Group** (Weeks 3, 7, 11): Collaborative activities

### Feedback Sessions (Even Weeks: 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12, 14)
Present work and receive constructive feedback from peers and instructor.

See [[Studio-Session-Guide]] and [[Feedback-Session-Guide]] for details.
