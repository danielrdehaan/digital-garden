# Lecture - Week 10: Mastering Basics

## Overview

Mastering is the final step before your music reaches listeners. This week we demystify the mastering process and learn techniques for preparing competitive, professional-sounding masters.

## Topics

### What Is Mastering?
- The role of mastering in the production chain
- What mastering can and can't fix
- DIY mastering vs. professional mastering
- When to master yourself vs. hire out

### Loudness and Dynamics
- Understanding LUFS, RMS, and peak levels
- Platform loudness normalization
- The loudness war (and why we lost)
- Preserving dynamics while achieving loudness

### The Mastering Chain
- Metering and analysis first
- EQ for tonal balance
- Compression and limiting
- Stereo imaging
- Final limiting and ceiling

### Practical Workflow
- Reference track comparison
- A/B testing your master
- Multiple format exports
- Metadata and delivery specs

## In-Class Activities

1. **Loudness Analysis** (15 min)
   - Analyze several commercial tracks for LUFS
   - Compare streaming service targets

2. **Basic Mastering Chain** (30 min)
   - Apply a mastering chain to a mixdown
   - Focus on subtle, musical adjustments

3. **A/B Comparison** (15 min)
   - Compare your master to a reference
   - Identify differences and adjust
