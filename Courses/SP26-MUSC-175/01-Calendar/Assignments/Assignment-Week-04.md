# Assignment - Week 04: Mixing Deep Dive + Habits

**Due:** Sunday, February 22, 2026 @ 11:59 PM

## Overview

Continue developing your mix from Week 3, applying the compression and EQ techniques covered this week.

## Deliverables

Submit to Canvas:

### 1. Revised Mix

Update your Week 3 mix to include:
- At least 3 elements with compression (explain each)
- Subtractive EQ on at least 3 elements
- High-pass filtering where appropriate
- At least one creative effect (reverb, delay, etc.)

### 2. Plugin Chain Documentation

For **3 key elements** (e.g., kick, vocal, bass), provide:
- Screenshot of the plugin chain
- Explanation of each plugin's purpose
- Before/after audio clips (optional but encouraged)

**Example format:**
```
Element: Lead Vocal
Chain: EQ → Compressor → De-esser → Reverb Send
- EQ: Cut 200Hz mud, boost 3kHz presence
- Compressor: 4:1 ratio, medium attack for punch
- De-esser: Targeting 6kHz sibilance
- Reverb: Plate, 1.5s decay, subtle blend
```

### 3. A/B Comparison

Export two versions:
- `LASTNAME_Week04_V1.wav` - Your Week 3 mix (before this week's changes)
- `LASTNAME_Week04_V2.wav` - Your revised mix

Write a brief comparison (100-150 words):
- What improved?
- What would you still change?

### 4. Habit Check-In

Continue your weekly habit documentation:
- Completion record
- Reflection on progress
- Evidence of practice

## Rubric

| Criteria | Excellent (90-100%) | Good (80-89%) | Adequate (70-79%) | Needs Work (<70%) |
|----------|---------------------|---------------|-------------------|-------------------|
| **Processing** | Thoughtful, musical compression and EQ | Good processing with minor issues | Basic processing | Inappropriate or missing processing |
| **Documentation** | Clear chains with strong rationale | Good documentation | Basic documentation | Missing or unclear |
| **Improvement** | Clear improvement from Week 3 | Noticeable improvement | Some improvement | No clear improvement |
| **Habit Check-In** | Complete and insightful | Adequate | Basic | Missing or minimal |

## Tips

- Listen to compression in context, not solo
- Small EQ moves often work better than large ones
- Compare to your reference track frequently
- If it sounds worse, undo and try something different
