# Assignment - Week 11: Collaborative Project

**Due:** Sunday, April 19, 2026 @ 11:59 PM

## Overview

Work with a partner or group to create a collaborative musical piece. Document the collaboration process and reflect on the experience.

## Deliverables

Submit to Canvas:

### 1. Collaborative Piece

Create a piece together with your collaborator(s):

**Requirements:**
- At least 30 seconds of music
- Each person contributes distinct elements
- The contributions should work together cohesively

**Collaboration can happen via:**
- Same-room session (in class or outside)
- Remote stem exchange
- Real-time online collaboration

### 2. Your Contribution

Export and submit your individual contribution:
- Stems of what you created
- Brief description of your role

### 3. Final Piece

Submit the completed collaborative piece:
- Bounced audio file
- Name: `TEAM_Week11_Collab.wav` (include all names)

### 4. Collaboration Reflection

Write an individual reflection (250-350 words):

**Address these questions:**
- How did you decide on direction/division of labor?
- What was your communication process like?
- What challenges did you face in collaborating?
- What did you learn from your collaborator(s)?
- What would you do differently next time?
- How does this compare to working alone?

### 5. Habit Check-In

Continue your weekly habit documentation.

## Rubric

| Criteria | Excellent (90-100%) | Good (80-89%) | Adequate (70-79%) | Needs Work (<70%) |
|----------|---------------------|---------------|-------------------|-------------------|
| **Collaboration** | Clear contributions from all, cohesive result | Good collaboration | Basic collaboration | Unclear or unequal contributions |
| **Musical Result** | Polished, musical, works well together | Good musical result | Basic result | Poor or incomplete |
| **Reflection** | Insightful, honest, detailed process analysis | Good reflection | Basic reflection | Minimal reflection |
| **Habit Check-In** | Complete and insightful | Adequate | Basic | Missing or minimal |

## Finding a Collaborator

**Options:**
- Pair up in class (Wednesday studio session)
- Connect via Canvas discussion
- Work with someone outside the class (must document)

If you cannot find a collaborator, contact the instructor ASAP.

## Tips

- Communicate expectations early
- Establish a shared tempo and key
- Save versions frequently
- Be open to ideas that differ from yours
