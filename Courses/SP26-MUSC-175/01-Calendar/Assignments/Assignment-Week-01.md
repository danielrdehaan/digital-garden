# Assignment - Week 01: Design Two Weekly Habits

**Due:** Sunday, February 1, 2026 @ 11:59 PM

## Overview

This week you'll design the two habits that will guide your practice throughout the semester. Choose thoughtfully—these habits will shape your growth and become the foundation of your Final Project.

## Deliverables

Submit to Canvas:

### 1. Habit Design Document (Written)

For **each of your two habits**, provide:

**Habit Statement** (fill in the blanks):
> "I will [SPECIFIC ACTION] for [TIME/AMOUNT] [WHEN/HOW OFTEN]."

**Skill Area:** (Production, Creative, or Technical)

**Why this habit?** (2-3 sentences)
- Why did you choose this habit?
- How does it connect to your goals as a musician?

**What does success look like?**
- How will you know you completed the habit?
- What evidence will you create?

**Potential obstacles:**
- What might get in the way?
- How will you address these challenges?

### 2. Baseline Documentation

For **each habit**, document your current skill level:

- Screenshots, audio recordings, or video showing where you are now
- This becomes your "before" evidence for the Final Project
- Be honest—the point is to show growth over time!

### 3. Habit Tracker Setup

Show how you'll track your habits:
- Spreadsheet, calendar, app screenshot, photos, etc.
- Include your planned schedule

## Rubric

| Criteria | Excellent (90-100%) | Good (80-89%) | Adequate (70-79%) | Needs Work (<70%) |
|----------|---------------------|---------------|-------------------|-------------------|
| **Habit Design** | Specific, measurable habits with clear timing; thoughtful rationale | Clear habits with good rationale | Habits are somewhat vague; basic rationale | Vague or missing habit statements |
| **Baseline Documentation** | Clear, honest documentation of current skill level | Adequate baseline shown | Minimal baseline documentation | Missing or unclear baseline |
| **Planning** | Detailed tracker setup; obstacles identified with solutions | Good planning shown | Basic planning | Little evidence of planning |

## Tips for Success

- **Be specific:** "Practice mixing for 20 minutes" not "Get better at mixing"
- **Start small:** You can always increase later
- **Be honest:** Your baseline doesn't need to impress—it needs to be accurate
- **Choose what excites you:** You'll be doing these habits all semester
