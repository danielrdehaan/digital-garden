# Feedback Session - Week 14

## Session Focus

Final portfolio feedback session. Present your near-complete portfolios for last-round peer feedback before final submission.

## Session Structure

| Time | Activity |
|------|----------|
| 0:00-0:05 | Setup and announcements |
| 0:05-1:10 | Portfolio presentations + feedback |
| 1:10-1:20 | Final submission reminders |

## What to Present

Share your **near-complete portfolio**:

- Walk through your portfolio organization
- Share your written reflection highlights
- Show key before/after evidence
- Present or preview your demonstration

## Presentation Format

- **5-6 minutes** to present
- **2-3 minutes** for feedback
- Focus on what you want feedback on before final submission

## Feedback Focus Areas

This is your **last chance for peer feedback**! Ask for input on:

- Is the growth/improvement clear?
- Does the reflection effectively tell your story?
- Is the organization logical and easy to follow?
- What's missing or could be stronger?
- Is the presentation/demonstration engaging?

## Peer Feedback Is Valuable

Take this session seriously—both giving and receiving:

- **Receiving:** Take notes on every suggestion
- **Giving:** Be specific and constructive
- **Everyone:** This helps all of us improve before final submission

## Checklist Review

Use this session to verify you have:

- [ ] Written reflection (500-750 words)
- [ ] Before/After evidence for both habits
- [ ] Process documentation
- [ ] Final demonstration ready
- [ ] Files organized and named clearly

## After Today

- Implement feedback received
- Finalize all materials
- Test your submission before Friday
- **Final submission: Friday, May 16 @ 11:59 PM**
