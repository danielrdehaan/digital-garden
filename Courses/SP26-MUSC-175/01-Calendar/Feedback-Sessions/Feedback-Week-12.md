# Feedback Session - Week 12

## Session Focus

Present collaborative projects from Week 11. Discuss the collaboration process and what you learned from working with others.

## Session Structure

| Time | Activity |
|------|----------|
| 0:00-0:05 | Setup and announcements |
| 0:05-1:10 | Group presentations + feedback |
| 1:10-1:20 | Collaboration reflections |

## What to Present

Each **collaboration group** presents together:

- Play your collaborative creation (60-90 seconds)
- Each member explains their contribution
- Discuss your collaboration process
- Share what worked and what was challenging

## Presentation Format

- **5-7 minutes** per group (multiple members presenting)
- **3-4 minutes** for feedback
- All group members should speak

## Feedback Focus Areas

When giving feedback on collaborative work:

- How well do the individual contributions fit together?
- What's the overall vibe/cohesion of the piece?
- What creative choices stand out?
- How might the piece develop further?

## Process Questions to Address

During your presentation, discuss:

1. How did you decide on direction/genre?
2. How did you divide responsibilities?
3. What was your communication like?
4. What would you do differently next time?
5. What did you learn from your collaborators?

## Individual Reflection

Even though you're presenting as a group, consider your personal takeaways:

- What did collaboration teach you about your own process?
- How did working with others challenge or inspire you?
- Would you collaborate with this group again?
