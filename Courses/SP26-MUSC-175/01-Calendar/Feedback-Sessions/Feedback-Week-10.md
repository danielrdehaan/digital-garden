# Feedback Session - Week 10

## Session Focus

Present your live performance setups from Week 9. Demonstrate your Session View organization and clip launching approach.

## Session Structure

| Time | Activity |
|------|----------|
| 0:00-0:05 | Setup and announcements |
| 0:05-1:10 | Student presentations + feedback |
| 1:10-1:20 | Wrap-up and performance tips |

## What to Present

Share your **live performance setup** from Week 9:

- Walk through your Session View organization
- Demonstrate launching clips/scenes
- Show how you'd perform a section of your set
- Discuss your organizational strategy

## Presentation Format

- **4-5 minutes** to present (including demonstration)
- **2-3 minutes** for feedback
- Connect to main audio system for playback

## Performance Presentation Tips

Since this is performance-focused:
- Actually perform a bit, don't just talk!
- Show what happens when you launch clips
- Demonstrate any live effects or manipulations
- It's okay if it's not perfect—that's real performance

## Feedback Focus Areas

When giving feedback on performance setups:

- Is the organization logical and easy to navigate?
- How intuitive is the clip arrangement?
- What could make the performance more dynamic?
- Are there any technical issues to address?

## Discussion Questions

- How did you decide what to include?
- What was challenging about converting to Session View?
- How much is scripted vs. improvised in your approach?
- What would you add with more time?

## Final Project Connection

Remember: Your live performance setup could be evidence for your Final Project if one of your habits relates to performance or technical workflow!
