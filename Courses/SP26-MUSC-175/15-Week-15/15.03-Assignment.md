# Assignment - Week 15: Final Project Submission

**Due:** Sunday, May 17, 2026 @ 11:59 PM

## Overview

Submit your completed Evidence Portfolio showcasing your growth through the semester's habit practice.

## Final Submission Requirements

Submit to Canvas as a **single ZIP file or shared folder link**:

### Required Components

#### 1. Written Reflection (20% of Final Project grade)
- **500-750 words**
- Addresses your habits, journey, and learning
- Submitted as PDF
- Filename: `01_Written_Reflection.pdf`

#### 2. Evidence for Habit 1 (15% of Final Project grade)
- Baseline evidence (Weeks 1-2)
- Mid-semester evidence (Weeks 6-8)
- Final evidence (Weeks 13-15)
- Clear before/after comparison
- Folder: `02_Habit1_Evidence/`

#### 3. Evidence for Habit 2 (15% of Final Project grade)
- Baseline evidence (Weeks 1-2)
- Mid-semester evidence (Weeks 6-8)
- Final evidence (Weeks 13-15)
- Clear before/after comparison
- Folder: `03_Habit2_Evidence/`

#### 4. Process Documentation (25% of Final Project grade)
- Selected weekly check-ins (minimum 5)
- Habit tracker or log
- Additional supporting materials
- Folder: `04_Process_Documentation/`

#### 5. Final Demonstration (25% of Final Project grade)
- Video presentation (3-5 minutes) OR
- Presentation materials used in class
- Shows your growth journey with evidence
- Folder: `05_Final_Demonstration/`

### Submission Structure

```

│   ├── baseline_[description].[ext]

```
